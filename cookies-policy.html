01. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your actions and preferences over a period of time, so you don’t have to re-enter them whenever you return to the site.

02. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to operate and improve our website, personalize your experience, analyze site performance, and support our marketing efforts.
