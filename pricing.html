FAQ

Questions about pricing

How much does a website cost?

Website and landing-page retainers start at ₹29,999 per month on Starter, which covers one active project of up to five pages. Growth is ₹59,999 per month for three active projects of up to fifteen pages. Larger programmes are quoted individually.

Can I pay yearly instead of monthly?

Yes. Choosing yearly billing saves 15% against the monthly rate. Both are billed in advance for the period you select.

What is included in every plan?

UI/UX design, responsive development and SEO are in all three plans. Starter adds email support, Growth adds CMS integration and priority support, and Enterprise adds a dedicated team and ongoing maintenance.

What counts as an active project?

One stream of work in progress at a time — a website, a product build, or a defined piece of work on an existing platform. Starter covers one at once, Growth three, and Enterprise as many as you need running.

How do I price something that does not fit a plan?

Send us the brief through the project form and we will quote it directly. Enterprise is priced to your requirements rather than off a rate card.
